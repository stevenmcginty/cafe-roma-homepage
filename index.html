Our story

A warm corner of St Albans since 1997

A fresh chapter for a much-loved cafe, under new ownership since 2024.

Cafe Roma has been part of St Peter's Street since 1997, serving the St Albans community for nearly three decades. In 2024 Velina took over, bringing a decade of experience as a store manager at Costa and a genuine passion for great coffee and warm hospitality.

Under her care the cafe has a cosy atmosphere, genuinely warm service and proper espresso, with fresh energy and small touches that make every visit feel considered. Whether you are stopping in on the walk to work or settling in for the afternoon, you are welcome to stay a while.

A day at Roma

From the first americano to last orders

Weekdays we open at 06:30 and close at 17:30. The marker follows the real clock in St Albans.

Checking the clock…
06:30

Doors open

Grinder on, shutters up, first americano of the day.

08:00

The morning run

Bacon baps and almond croissants, mostly heading out of the door.

10:30

Mid-morning

Avocado toast, beans on toast, a second coffee and a seat by the window.

12:00

Lunch

Jacket potatoes, BLTs and fresh sandwiches, made to order.

14:30

Cake o'clock

Cannoli, carrot cake and Eccles cake, with a pot of Earl Grey.

16:30

Last orders

Milkshakes, gelato and the last of the pastries. Beans by the bag to take Roma home.
